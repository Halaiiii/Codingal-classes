Health benefits

Eating fruits provides vital nutrients like vitamins, minerals, and dietary fiber. Regular intake helps lower the risk of heart disease, stroke, and diabetes, supports healthy digestion, boosts the immune system, and aids in weight management.

NOTE

  1. Fruits are a rich source of antioxidants that help protect the body from harmful free radicals.
  2. They are low in calories and high in water content, making them an excellent choice for hydration and weight control.
  3. Consuming a variety of fruits ensures a diverse intake of essential nutrients for overall health.
